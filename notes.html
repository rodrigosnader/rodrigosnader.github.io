Smaller thoughts that didn't deserve a full essay.

  • Apr 09

    After trying asyncio on three agent projects, I went back to threads. The ergonomics more than pay for any overhead people warn you about.

  • Apr 05

    Debugging tool calls without losing your mind: a wrapper that logs args, return value, and latency to JSONL. Thirty lines, saves hours.

  • Mar 30

    Not every "the model hallucinates" problem is the model. Most of the time it's an ambiguous system prompt. Rewrite the prompt before you blame the weights.

  • Mar 21

    Tried building agent memory with vector search + a reranker. Concluded that a well-structured prompt beats it for 80% of cases. The other 20% are when you really do have tens of thousands of memories.

  • Mar 14

    A good tool definition reads like a well-written function signature. If the model can't figure out how to call it by looking at the name and the args, you haven't named it well enough.

  • Mar 05

    The best eval I've ever written was three lines of Python that checked if a substring was in the output. It caught more regressions than any rubric-based judge.

  • Feb 28

    The price of adding another abstraction layer is always paid at 3 AM, in production, during an incident. Don't forget that when deciding whether to add it.
