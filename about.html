About

I'm Rodrigo. I build software — mostly applied AI at the day job, smaller experiments at night — and I write here about whatever I've been turning over in my head.

The rhythm is usually the same. I get curious about something. The explanations I find don't quite land. I argue with them until something clicks. Then I write the post I wish I'd found in the first place.

So this blog is downstream of curiosity, not credentials. Most of what I write about — quantum mechanics, cosmology, free will, Bayesian reasoning — I have no official authority on. I'm just someone who couldn't move on until he understood it well enough to explain it.

Things I run

  • JournalClub.io — an automated stream of AI paper summaries on Instagram. It picks fresh papers, turns them into short reels, and posts daily without me touching it. The 27 most recent are listed below.
  • This blog — plain HTML, no framework, no build step. The interactive figures are vanilla canvas. I want it to still be readable in ten years without anyone maintaining it.
  • Day-job AI — agent infrastructure and automation. The unglamorous plumbing that turns "demo" into "deployed".

Things I'm into

Physics — especially quantum mechanics, where the math is small and the implications won't sit still. Cosmology, slowly, the way someone might read poetry in a language they're still learning. Quantum computing, more out of curiosity than belief in any particular timeline. Rationality and Bayesian reasoning, ever since they broke the way I update beliefs and I couldn't go back. Computational neuroscience whenever it brushes against AI. Game theory when politics gets weird enough to feel like a coordination problem.

If you find me deep in a rabbit hole, it's probably one of these.

The site itself

Intentionally boring. No framework, no build step, no CMS. Just HTML, CSS, and a small amount of vanilla JavaScript for the interactive figures and the theme toggle. View source — everything is inline, nothing is obfuscated.
