About

I'm Rodrigo S. Nader. I work on applied AI and product — currently building agent and automation tools for teams that want to move fast without becoming prisoners of their own stack.

Before this, I spent years in the usual places: startups where the roadmap changed every Monday, bigger companies where the roadmap never changed at all, and a few good and bad choices in between. Most of what I write here comes from that zigzag.

I write because it's the best way I know to think slowly about things that happened fast. When a post helps you — or disagrees with something you believed — tell me. That's the metric I actually care about.

What you'll find here

Three kinds of posts, loosely:

  • Essays — longer pieces on ideas I want to get straight in my head. Sometimes opinionated, sometimes interactive, always slow-cooked.
  • Notes — short observations from day-to-day work. A paragraph, maybe two.
  • Links — writing by other people that I think is worth your time.

The site itself

This site is intentionally boring. No framework, no build step, no CMS. Just HTML, CSS, and a small amount of vanilla JavaScript for the interactive figures and the theme toggle. I like knowing that it'll still be readable in ten years without anyone maintaining it.

If you want to see how the interactive figures are built, view source. Everything is inline. Nothing is obfuscated.
