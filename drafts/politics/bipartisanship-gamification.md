A few insights from the article that could refine your idea:

"Exploitation by free riders" – Some parties might exploit the system by approving minor, symbolic bipartisan measures while still obstructing real cooperation.
Solution: Use a weighted metric where deeper, structural reforms count more than surface-level agreements.
"The shadow of the future" – Long-term incentives matter more than short-term gains.
Solution: The funding should not just be tied to recent votes but also to sustained patterns of bipartisan cooperation over multiple terms.
"Punishing defectors" – Right now, there’s no cost to extreme obstructionism.
